A desktop app for recording and viewing your video logs. Yap is a fast way to capture your day-to-day thoughts and experiences.

## Download

Download for macOS, Windows, and Linux from the [releases page](https://github.com/felipap/yap/releases/latest).

## Sharing with AI

Yap lets you transcribe and summarize your videos. Trascription requires an
OpenAI API key, and summarization requires a Gemini API key.

## Privacy

Yap is fully local. Sentry is used for error reporting, but

Yap is fully local and doesn't collect any data from you. All your data stays in your machine.
